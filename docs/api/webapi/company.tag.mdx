A Company represents an organizational entity used to logically group multiple customers. Associating customers with a Company enables centralized management of related data such as delivery addresses, discount rules, and custom business logic.

This is particularly useful for reporting, invoicing, and applying shared configurations across customers that belong to the same organization.
