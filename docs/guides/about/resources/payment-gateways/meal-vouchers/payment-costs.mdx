# Voucherly and payment costs

Accepting meal vouchers with Voucherly adds a single item to the merchant's costs: the Voucherly service cost.
The payment method fees and the terms of the agreements with the issuers stay as they are today.

:::info
This cost model applies to the **Voucherly Buoni Pasto eCommerce** service.
It does not apply to **Voucherly Orchestration Platform** or to the other Voucherly services.
:::

<MealVoucherCosts />

## What stays the same

- The fees of every payment method, from cards to PayPal to the other active methods, charged by the gateways the merchant already uses.
- The economic terms of the agreement with each issuer, which remains a direct relationship between merchant and issuer.

## What is added

- The Voucherly service cost, a monthly subscription set according to volumes.
- The subscription covers the connection with the issuers and the features Voucherly provides: the Dashboard with the reporting of payments, the customer care tools, the Wallet for customers' prepaid credit and the other features of the platform.

## Who charges what

- The payment gateways charge the fees on cards, PayPal and the other methods, as today.
- The issuer applies the fee set in the agreement and reimburses the merchant for the value of the vouchers.
- Voucherly invoices the monthly subscription to the merchant, with automatic debit.
