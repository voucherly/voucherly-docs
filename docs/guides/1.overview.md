# Overview

Voucherly provides a prebuilt, optimized payment environment that allows merchants to securely and effortlessly accept online payments, including meal vouchers, through a wide range of payment gateways.

With Voucherly, merchants no longer need to integrate each payment gateway individually on their websites. Voucherly handles all the complexities for you.

Customers are redirected to the Voucherly Checkout page, where they can choose from a variety of payment options—including meal voucher gateways—and complete their transactions quickly and securely. For a complete list of supported payment gateways, see [here](resources/payment-gateways#full-list-of-available-payment-gateways).

This guides is designed for developers looking to integrate Voucherly into a merchant's website.
