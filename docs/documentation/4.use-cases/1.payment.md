# Accept Payments

Accepting a payment allows merchants to initialize a new payment instance for a customer and manage the result.

Below, you will find the classic payment flow and steps for a seamless integration on the merchant's website.

## Payment Lifecycle

1. When the customer is ready to complete their purchase, the merchant's website must create a new payment using the [Create Payment API](/api/webapi/create-payment), setting `mode` to `Payment`.

2. The API response will include the URL of the Voucherly Checkout page to which the customer must be redirected.

3. The customer can use a voucher payment gateway, a non-voucher payment gateway, or a combination of both to complete the payment.

4. After the payment is completed, if the `callbackUrl` field was populated in the [Create Payment API](/api/webapi/create-payment) request, a [Server-to-Server (S2S)](/documentation/s2s) notification will be sent to the specified URL. The customer will then be redirected to the merchant's website, as specified in the request.

5. The final step is to call the [Confirm Payment API](/api/webapi/confirm-payment) to confirm all completed but unconfirmed transactions. This is typically done during order fulfillment. Note that this step is unnecessary if [Auto Confirm](/documentation/resources/payments#auto-confirm) is enabled in the Dashboard.

## Restrict Available Payment Gateways

Merchants can dynamically specify which payment gateways are available for each payment by providing an array of payment gateway IDs in the [Create Payment API](/api/webapi/create-payment) request (see [here](/documentation/resources/payment-gateways) for a full list).

If you do not require this feature, leave the `PaymentGateways` field empty and manage gateway activation/deactivation from the Dashboard.

## Wallet Charge During Payment

On the Voucherly Checkout page, if Wallet functionality is enabled, customers can top up their wallet credit to use for current or future payments.

This process is entirely managed by Voucherly and requires no additional integration from the merchant.

If a wallet charge occurs within the payment flow, a new payment object will be created with:
- `Mode` set to `Wallet`.
- `ParentPaymentId` referencing the original payment.

## Complete or Refund a Payment

A payment may consist of multiple transactions across different payment gateways, with varying outcomes (some successful, others not).

As detailed in [this guide](/documentation/resources/payments), after the customer completes the payment and is redirected to the merchant's website, successful transactions may have a status of either `PAID` or `CONFIRMED`. This depends on factors like the type of payment gateway or whether [Auto Confirm](/documentation/resources/payments#auto-confirm) is enabled.

:::warning
Transactions completed through voucher payment gateways automatically transition to the `CONFIRMED` status.
:::

Merchants must confirm or refund payments. Leaving a payment in the `PAID` status could result in the funds being returned to the customer, depending on the gateway configuration. Always confirm successful payments, for example, when the order is prepared or shipped.

## Quick Guide

Let’s walk through a simple example of integrating the payment flow from scratch.

### 1. Create Payment

:::info
Refer to the [Create Payment API](/api/webapi/create-payment) for detailed functionality and usage.
:::

When the customer is ready to finalize their purchase, the merchant's website should make an HTTP call to the [Create Payment API](/api/webapi/create-payment).

In the request:
- Set `mode` to `Payment`.
- Include customer data or `customerId` (if returning).
- Provide `redirectOkUrl` and `redirectKoUrl` for post-payment redirection.
- Use the `lines` field to detail items in the purchase. For each item, the `isFood` field determines whether it can be paid with vouchers.

Optional fields:
- `referenceId` for reconciliation with internal systems (e.g., cart ID, order ID).
- `callbackUrl` for S2S notifications.

:::warning
Save the customer ID generated by Voucherly during the first payment. For future payments, provide only the `CustomerId` unless customer details need updating.
:::

### 2. Set Up a Checkout Callback Page

When the customer completes payment on the Voucherly Checkout page, they are redirected to the URLs specified in the [Create Payment API](/api/payments/create_payment) request:
- If the payment was successful, the customer is redirected to the `redirectOkUrl`.
- If the payment failed, the customer is redirected to the `redirectKoUrl`.

To handle the payment result, you need to define two routes on the merchant's website that process the query string parameters sent with the redirection:

- **success**: Indicates the payment result (`OK` for success, `KO` for failure).
- **status**: The status of the payment.
- **paymentId**: The unique ID of the payment in the format `pay_XXXXXXXXXXX`.
- **referenceId**: The merchant's custom reference ID sent during payment creation.
- **amount**: The total paid amount in cents.
- **voucherAmount**: The portion of the paid amount covered by vouchers in cents.
- **walletAmount**: The portion of the paid amount covered by wallet credit in cents.
- **transactions**: The number of transactions involved in the payment.
- **customerId**: The unique Voucherly identifier for the customer in the format `cs_XXXXXXXXXXX`. This must be stored on the merchant's system for future payments.
- **customerFirstName**: The customer's first name.
- **customerLastName**: The customer's last name.
- **customerEmail**: The customer's email address.
- **tenant**: *live* or *sand*.

### 3. Set Up a Server-to-Server Endpoint (Optional)

It is highly recommended to configure a Server-to-Server (S2S) endpoint on the merchant's system. This endpoint allows Voucherly to notify you of the payment result before redirecting the customer to your site. Refer to [this guide](s2s) for detailed instructions on implementing an S2S endpoint and its behavior.

:::warning
As described in the [Server-to-Server section](/documentation/s2s), ensure that your endpoint responds correctly to S2S notifications. Failure to do so may result in the payment being canceled and the funds refunded.
:::

### 4. Confirm Payment

:::info
Refer to the [Confirm Payment API](/api/payments/confirm-payment) for detailed functionality and usage.
:::

:::warning
If you prefer immediate confirmation of payments without a two-step process, you can enable Auto Confirm in the Dashboard and skip this step.
:::

Payment confirmation typically occurs when the order is accepted by the merchant. To confirm a payment, call the [Confirm Payment API](/api/webapi/confirm-payment).

If the order cannot be fulfilled or the customer cancels before shipment, use the [Refund Payment API](/api/webapi/refund-payment). Voucherly will:
- Automatically cancel transactions in the `PAID` status.
- Refund transactions in the `CONFIRMED` status.
